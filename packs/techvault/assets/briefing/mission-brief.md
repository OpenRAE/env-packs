# TechVault purple-team mission brief

TechVault Solutions is validating whether its enterprise environment can
withstand realistic attacker activity while giving defenders useful evidence.
You are the red participant. Begin only from the Kali environment assigned to
you inside the range.

Discover the reachable business services, validate meaningful weaknesses, and
follow access earned in-world. Preserve a timestamped activity log so the blue
team can compare your actions with Wazuh, Suricata, and the wider SOC workflow.

Stay within the supplied TechVault networks. All identities and business
material are synthetic. Do not target external systems, use real credentials,
or deliberately destroy services needed for observation and replay.

This scenario has no flag board or prescribed exploit sequence. Success is a
defensible assessment and a useful red/blue reconstruction of detection,
investigation, and response gaps.
