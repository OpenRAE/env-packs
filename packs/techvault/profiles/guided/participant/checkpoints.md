# Guided checkpoints

## Exposure

Explain which TechVault interfaces are reachable from Kali and why you chose
your first target.

## Initial weakness

Show the difference between expected portal behavior and the weakness you
validated, using non-destructive evidence.

## Internal context

Describe the identity or data relationships you learned from in-world access.

## Detection comparison

Compare your activity log with defender observations. Identify at least one
well-observed action and one visibility gap.
