# Guided hints

- Begin with service discovery, not assumptions about the company topology.
- Authentication failures can reveal whether input is handled safely without
  requiring destructive payloads.
- A business application connected to a database or directory often exposes
  more useful trust relationships than its landing page suggests.
- File shares and workstations are useful both as targets and as sources of
  context about identities and business operations.
- After each meaningful action, ask the blue team what Wazuh or Suricata saw.
