# Rules of engagement

- Stay within the TechVault range and its assigned network space.
- Start from the participant-accessible Kali environment.
- Treat all people, accounts, mail addresses, and business data as synthetic.
- Do not target external services, infrastructure, or real identities.
- Avoid destructive changes that would prevent the blue team from observing or
  replaying the exercise.
- Record commands, timestamps, and observations for the joint debrief.
- Stop and notify the facilitator if a route appears to leave the range.
