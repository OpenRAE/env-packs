# Code of Conduct

Keep project discussions clear, kind, and focused on the work. Good-faith
disagreement is welcome; making people feel unwelcome is not.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good faith, and give it.
- Keep it on topic: environment-pack format, tooling, and their use.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory or
sexualized language, and sustained disruption.

## Scope

This applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, and any other
channel that represents the project — and when you represent the project
elsewhere.

## Report a concern

Email **security@autarchy.ai** with what happened. Reports go only to the
maintainer and are handled privately. Do not open a public issue for a conduct
concern.

This is a single-maintainer project. There is no separate enforcement group, and
the same person who maintains the code reviews conduct reports. The maintainer
may remove comments, commits, issues, and pull requests that break this policy,
and may block a contributor who does so repeatedly.
